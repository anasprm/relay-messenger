How it works

A simple loop that keeps you moving

StudyDesk is built around three habits: read a short lesson, practise it, then review it later. That is the whole system.

  1. 1Read one lessonTen to fifteen minutes, with worked examples and a short summary at the end.
  2. 2Practise immediatelyA small problem set for the same topic, so the idea is used before it fades.
  3. 3Review on a scheduleFlashcards bring the topic back at the right time instead of all at once.
